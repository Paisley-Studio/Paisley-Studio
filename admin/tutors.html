Tutor Accounts
Create, suspend and manage tutor logins
Creating a tutor account: you set their name, email, and password. The moment you click Create tutor, the password is shown once in a pop-up — copy it or take a screenshot and send it to the tutor.

For their safety, it is not stored anywhere and will never be shown again. If you lose it, click the key icon on the tutor row and the tutor will receive a link by email to set a new one.

They sign in at /tutor/{slug} — a personal link you can send them. To attach students, click Manage students on any tutor row. The tutor sees only their own students, their own posts, and the work you ask them to help mark. You remain in charge.
