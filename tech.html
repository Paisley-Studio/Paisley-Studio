Good to know

Questions students and parents ask

The questions that matter most about the bootcamp, the price, the AI rules, and how mastery actually works.

Does my child need coding experience to join Paisley TECH?

No prior coding experience is needed. Paisley TECH takes learners from novice to mastery. It starts at DISCOVER — the very first level — where learners explore what AI can and cannot do. A coach guides every step, and by the end of the bootcamp most learners are building real projects independently.

When does the Festive Creator Bootcamp start and end?

Orientation and class introduction happen on 31 October. Official learning and studio building begin on 1 November and run until 12 December, finishing with an online graduation where the best projects are crowned and certificates are awarded.

Can my child join late?

Yes. Learners can join anytime. Paisley TECH coaches help every late joiner catch up and keep up, so they can still reach mastery no matter where they start.

What will students actually learn?

Eight disciplines: Coding, AI, Writing, Research, Design, Media, Music and Entrepreneurship. Learners build real things such as websites, games, blogs, portfolios and graphic designs. Every discipline runs on the same seven-level ladder and every discipline uses AI. The studio also teaches how to use AI honestly: each submission carries an AI-use reflection showing which tools were used, what they were used for, and what the learner contributed.

How are learners assessed?

Assessment and progress are project-based. Learners build real-world projects, submit them to a coach, receive written feedback, revise, and earn mastery approvals. Mastery, not just opening lessons, unlocks the next level, so learners should be ready to build real projects.

How does Paisley TECH work day-to-day?

The loop is: learn, create, submit, review, revise, master, share. Learners open a mission, use the built-in Writing Studio, Code Lab or Upload Pad to make their work, submit it to their coach with an AI-use reflection, receive written feedback, revise if needed, then receive a mastery approval.

What is the parent portal?

The interactive parent portal lets parents see their child's ideas and projects take shape in real time, follow progress towards mastery, and share finished projects with friends and family.

Is this safe? How is AI use managed?

Every AI tool a learner can open is first approved by a coach and added to the AI Toolbox. Each mission attaches specific tools and specific rules, shown to the learner before they begin. No external AI APIs and no API keys are used — learners open approved tools in a new tab. Every submission carries a mandatory AI-use reflection. The AI Toolbox is visible to parents on request.

Is Paisley TECH online?

Yes. Paisley TECH is 100% online, with a real human coach who reads every submission and provides written feedback.

What are the levels, and how does a learner actually move up?

Seven levels: DISCOVER, MAKE, BUILD, SOLVE, CREATE, INVENT, STUDIO. Progress is per-discipline, not blended — a learner can be BUILD in Coding while still DISCOVER in Music. A coach approves mastery after reading the actual work and the AI reflection. Approval unlocks the next level for that discipline.

Who is Paisley TECH for?

Curious kids and teens, roughly age 9–17, who want to make real things with AI. Whether they lean toward coding, design, stories, music, research or inventing a product, one of the eight disciplines will fit them. No prior coding experience is needed for any of them.

What will my child walk away with at the end of the bootcamp?

A certificate, a personal portfolio of coach-reviewed real-world projects, a visible mastery path across the disciplines they worked on, a chance to have their best project crowned at the online graduation, at least one build eligible for the public Tech Wall (only after coach approval, shown with first name only), and a working understanding of how to use AI as a creative partner honestly and safely.

How is the bootcamp structured?

Three phases on the road to mastery. Foundation: DISCOVER and MAKE — first project with guided AI use, first AI reflection, first coach review. Craft: BUILD and SOLVE — independent builds across at least two disciplines, first mastery approval, first showcase candidate. Studio: CREATE, INVENT and STUDIO — an original cross-discipline project, a public showcase after coach approval, and a personal portfolio. Learners move through phases by mastery, not by the calendar.

How does Paisley decide what's good enough to publish?

Nothing is published automatically. Every public project goes through coach approval first. Private projects stay private. Family-share links are generated by the learner but visible only to whoever has the link. Public showcases show a first name only. A parent can withdraw a public showcase at any time.

What tools does a learner use, and can I see them?

Approved AI tools live in the AI Toolbox — a coach-curated list. Each tool has a description, age guidance, permitted uses, restrictions, safety notes and disclosure requirements. Missions attach specific tools with specific rules. Writing, code, images, audio and video can also be uploaded directly. Every tool a learner has used appears in their AI-use reflection.

How much does Paisley TECH cost?

The Festive Creator Bootcamp costs USD 50 (about KES 6,500). Cohorts are small and places are limited.

How do I get started?

Select Start today, create a free sign-in on the Paisley TECH page, and choose a mission. Your child's journey begins the day the account is approved, and you can join anytime.
