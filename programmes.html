FAQ

Questions about our programmes.

What programmes does Paisley Studio offer?

Paisley Studio offers three online programmes: IGCSE and A-Level preparation, CBC tuition for Grades 1 to 12, and AI-led digital literacy (Paisley TECH) for kids and teens.

Can my child study from outside Kenya?

Yes. All programmes are fully online, so learners can join live classes from any country with a stable internet connection.

Is CBC tuition suitable for every grade?

CBC tuition covers Grades 1 to 12, with revision lessons, tutor-marked work and parent tracking for each learner.

Does my child need coding experience for the AI programme?

No. Paisley TECH is beginner friendly. A friendly coach guides learners to think, create and build with AI, with no coding needed.

How do I enrol?

Choose a programme, press its enrol or explore button and follow the registration steps. You can also contact the team for help choosing the right fit.

Can my child take more than one programme?

Yes. Many families combine programmes, for example CBC tuition alongside AI digital literacy.

Not sure which one?

Talk to us. We will point you to the right fit.
