Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Paisley Studio.

Answers to the most common questions from parents and students.

What is the British Curriculum?
The British Curriculum, also known as the National Curriculum for England, is a structured educational framework used in England and internationally. It is divided into Key Stages, with clear learning objectives and assessments at each stage.
What Key Stages does Paisley Studio offer?
We currently offer Key Stage 2 (Years 3-6), Key Stage 3 (Years 7-9), Key Stage 4 (Years 10-11) leading to IGCSE examinations, and Key Stage 5 (Years 12-13) leading to A-Level examinations.
How do online classes work?
Classes are conducted live via video conferencing with qualified tutors. Students receive class notes, assignments, and marked work. All you need is a computer, internet connection, camera, and microphone.
What is Key Stage 5?
Key Stage 5 covers Years 12-13 (ages 16-18) and prepares students for Cambridge International A-Level examinations. It's a two-year programme of advanced study designed for university entrance.
What subjects are available?
Core subjects include English, Mathematics, Science, and Computing. At KS4, students can choose from additional options including History, Geography, Modern Foreign Languages, and the Arts. At KS5, A-Level subjects include English Literature, Further Mathematics, Sciences, Humanities, Economics, Business Studies, Psychology, and more.
What A-Level subjects do you offer?
We offer a wide range of A-Level subjects including English Literature, Mathematics, Further Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, History, Geography, Economics, Business Studies, Psychology, Modern Foreign Languages, and Art & Design.
Who are the tutors?
Our tutors are qualified, experienced professionals who are trained in the British Curriculum. They provide expert guidance and personalised support to ensure every student succeeds.
How do I enrol my child?
Simply explore our Key Stages, select the appropriate year group, review the subject options, and get in touch via our contact form or visit our Enrolment page. Our team will guide you through the enrollment process.
