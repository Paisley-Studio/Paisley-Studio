FAQ

CBC tuition questions parents ask

Everything you need to know before getting started.

Which grades does CBC tuition cover?

Paisley Studio CBC tuition covers Lower Primary (Grades 1–3), Upper Primary (Grades 4–6), Junior School (Grades 7–9) and Senior School (Grades 10–12).

What is included in Paisley Studio CBC tuition?

Revision lessons and resources for every subject you take, work marked by live tutors with corrections, live classes with a clear timetable, a tutor on request, and results tracking with parents kept in the loop.

Do I need a tutor to join?

No. You can start with materials and assessment only, then request a tutor whenever you like. The Studio Desk approves and pairs you.

Who marks the work?

Live tutors mark submitted work and return it with feedback and corrections.

How do parents follow their child's progress?

Results are tracked in one place and parents are kept in the loop. One parent account shows every child, so you can switch between them easily.

Can I have children on different curriculums?

Yes. One parent account shows every child, whatever they study with us, and you simply switch between them.

How do I get started?

Create your account, choose your Grade and subjects, and add your parent or guardian. The Studio Desk then reviews your application and activates your account.

What are the technical requirements?

You will need a stable internet connection, a computer (Core i5 with 8GB RAM recommended), a webcam, and a microphone for live classes.

Ready to begin?

Join Paisley Studio today and give every learner the support, structure and materials they need to succeed.
