FAQ

Questions parents ask

Which grades does CBC tuition cover?

Lower Primary (Grades 1–3), Upper Primary (4–6), Junior School (7–9) and Senior School (10–12).

Do I need a tutor to join?

No. You can start with materials and assessment only, then request a tutor whenever you like. The Studio Desk approves and pairs you.

Can I have children on different curriculums?

Yes. One parent account shows every child, whether they study IGCSE online or CBC tuition, and you simply switch between them.

Who marks the work?

Live tutors mark submitted work and return it with feedback and corrections.

What are the technical requirements?

You will need a stable internet connection, a computer (Core i5, 8GB RAM recommended), a webcam, and a microphone for live classes.
